The test

  • 80 questions, 8:00 on the clock: about 6 seconds each.
  • 4 options per question. Press 1–4 or click.Tap one. Picking an answer moves on, and there's no going back.
  • Scoring: +1 right, wrong.

Optiver has never published the exact format. This follows the most common candidate reports: four options, +1 / −1, no skipping, and a pass line around 55 net. Some reports say −2 per wrong answer instead.
