What's inside

Classic Zetamac

Same rules and defaults: 2 minutes, answers accepted the instant they're right.

Find your weak spots

Every problem is timed and sorted by type: carries, borrows, each times table, 2-digit products.

Targeted drills

Rounds built from your slowest types, or the exact problems you keep missing.

80 in 8

80 multiple-choice questions in 8 minutes: decimals, fractions, missing numbers. Net scoring.

Goals

Set a target score and track how consistently you hit it, not just your best.

Optional sync

Keep phone and computer in step through your own private GitHub repository.

Your games are saved only on your device unless you turn on sync, which uses your own GitHub account.

Zetapro is an independent practice tool, not affiliated with Zetamac or Optiver.
